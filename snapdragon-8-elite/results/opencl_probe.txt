=> OpenCL is fully usable from user code on this device. libOpenCL.so is in
   /vendor/etc/public.libraries.txt, so even untrusted apps get it.
   Gotcha: do NOT set LD_LIBRARY_PATH when running OpenCL binaries from
   /data/local/tmp — it breaks the frontend's internal ICD dispatch
   (0 platforms / deadlock with a Khronos ICD loader in the path).
