=> GPU training WORKS on the Adreno 830 with NO workarounds — and converges
   bit-identically to CPU. Contrast with the Mali G925, which needed
   GGML_VK_DISABLE_COOPMAT=1 (coopmat training kernels emitted NaN) and still
   plateaued higher than CPU (0.30 vs 0.13). The Adreno has no coopmat path
   ("matrix cores: none"), so nothing to break; the plain f32 kernels are clean.
   (60 epochs of a 385-param MLP complete in <1 s on both backends —
   capability proof, not a speed comparison.)
